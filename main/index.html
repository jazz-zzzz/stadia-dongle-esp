LED Legend

  • Booting: soft white pulse
  • Scanning / no controller: dim blue heartbeat
  • Scanning + Wi-Fi AP on: blue-yellow alternating heartbeat
  • 1 controller in gamepad mode: green heartbeat
  • 2 controllers in gamepad mode: faster green heartbeat
  • PC sleeping: dim purple heartbeat
  • Mouse mode active: orange heartbeat
  • Error: red pulsing
  • OTA update: purple/yellow animation
  • Remote wake sent: brief white flash
